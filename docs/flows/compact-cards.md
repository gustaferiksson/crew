# Compact cards: the transcript shows a preview, the card opens in a sheet or popover (on the Mac, in place)

User decision (2026-11-01): A2UI cards in the transcript render like iMessage app messages, a compact preview that opens the full interactive card. Until now each card drew in full inline (a recipe is 600 pt or more), the newest one also opened from the pinned bar, and the same card could sit in two places with its controls (ticks, sliders, form fields) visible in both. Now the full card lives in one place, the card sheet, and the transcript and the bar are two ways to open it.

Apple's pattern: Messages draws an app message (Apple Pay, Fitness, a game, a poll) as a compact bubble with an icon, title and caption, and tapping it opens the full experience in a sheet. Crew follows it.

## The preview

- **Content**, from the live surface (`CardSummary`, also used by the pinned chip):
  - the 44 pt tinted symbol tile (32 pt with 8 pt corners on the Mac, see "The Mac" below) from the Header (`symbol`, `tone`; `rectangle.on.rectangle` and the accent colour when missing);
  - the title (Header `title`, else the first line of the first Text, else "Card from <bot>" / "Card"), `.headline`, up to 2 lines;
  - one summary line: the Header `subtitle`, `.subheadline`, secondary, 1 line; none when the card has no subtitle;
  - an optional trailing 44 pt thumbnail: the Header `image` (https, through `CachedImage`), rounded like the tile.
- **Shape:** the same glass card as before (`.regular` glass behind the content, 28 pt corners, 560 pt max width, leading), 12 pt padding (10 pt on the Mac), so it reads as the card in its closed state and keeps the transcript's rhythm. Light and dark come from the glass and semantic colours.
- **Tap:** opens the card sheet for that surface id (on the Mac a click expands the card in place instead). The whole preview is one button (`.plain` style): VoiceOver reads the title, the summary as its value, and "Shows the card" as its hint.
- **Context menu:** Expand Card / Collapse Card, then Pin Card / Unpin Card.
- **While a turn runs:** the preview stays tappable; the card inside the sheet is disabled while a turn runs, as before.
- **Card updates (`docs/flows/card-updates.md`):** unchanged rules. The "Updated" caption sits under the newest preview; older turns keep their one-line "updated below" pointer; update-only replies keep "Updated <title>". The preview reflects the live surface, so an update-only change of title or subtitle shows in it.
- **Streaming (progressive cards, #240):** the half-written card shows as this compact preview, titled from the partial surface (`CardSummary` of the preview model), dimmed and not tappable until the reply finishes, when the finished card's preview takes its place.
- **Small choice cards** (two or three buttons): also a preview, per the user's decision; answering takes one more tap. The tapped button's mark shows inside the sheet.
- **Search:** a hit inside a card's text highlights the turn; the card itself stays closed.
- **Thumbnail:** the Header hero image is cropped to fill the 44 pt square (`scaledToFill`), like Messages' link thumbnails.
- **Haptics and keys:** none on open (like the pinned chip). On the Mac the preview row is one button: Tab focuses it and Space expands or collapses the card.

## Expanding a card in place

User request (2026-11-01, after compact cards shipped): a preview can also expand into the full, interactive card right in the transcript and collapse back. On iPhone and iPad, opening the sheet and the pinned card stay exactly as they are; on the Mac expanding in place is the only way a card opens ("The Mac" below).

Apple's precedent is Reminders: a reminder with subtasks has a disclosure chevron of its own that shows them in place, while tapping the row itself opens the reminder for editing. Crew does the same: the row's tap keeps opening the sheet, a chevron beside it expands the card in place. The chevron is the HIG disclosure button (`chevron.down`, flipping to point up while expanded), not a trailing `chevron.right`, which on iOS reads as the row's drill-in indicator.

- **Affordance (iPhone, iPad; the Mac differs, see "The Mac"):** a trailing disclosure button in the preview row, after the thumbnail: `chevron.down`, `.footnote.weight(.semibold)`, secondary, rotating 180 degrees while expanded, with a 44 pt square hit area on iOS and 28 pt on the Mac (both `@ScaledMetric`), outside the row's open button. Tapping it toggles with `.smooth`; tapping anywhere else on the row still opens the sheet. The row's context menu gains Expand Card / Collapse Card (`chevron.down` / `chevron.up`) above Pin Card / Unpin Card. The context menu stays on the row only, so a long press lifts the row, not a screen-tall card, and long presses inside the expanded card's controls are theirs.
- **Expanded:** the same glass card grows: the preview row on top, unchanged, then the live surface below it (20 pt sides and bottom as in the sheet and the pre-compact inline card, 8 pt from the row). The row already is the header (symbol, title, subtitle, thumbnail), so the inline surface hides exactly the Header node `CardSummary` picked, matched by its component id through an environment value (`hiddenHeader`); any other Header (nested, in a template) still draws. A hero image therefore shows only as the row's thumbnail inline; the sheet still shows the whole Header (on the Mac, which has no sheet, the hero is only ever the row's thumbnail, see below). A card without a Header takes its title from its first Text, which then also shows inline under the row (accepted). The iPad popover anchor stays the row's frame, not the expanded card's.
- **The card inline is the real card:** its buttons send the same hidden action as the sheet, it is disabled while a turn runs (the chevron and Collapse stay enabled), and the tapped button's mark shows. Offline it behaves as the sheet does.
- **Same data, separate view state:** inline and sheet render the same `SurfaceViewModel` (`surfaces.processor.surfaceGroupModel[id]`), the same choice mark (`surfaces.choices[id]`) and the same owner id, so values never diverge: a field's text, a slider's value, a checklist tick or a choice made in one shows in the other. View state is per presentation, as in any two views of one document: a carousel's page, a map snapshot, text-field focus. On the Mac there is only the inline card.
- **Expanded state:** a `Set` of surface ids owned by `ChatView` (the screen container) and handed to rows through the environment like `openedCard`, so a lazy row recycled while scrolled away comes back expanded. Keyed by surface id: a follow-up that re-creates the same id ("make it vegan") keeps the card expanded at its new place, and the older turn keeps its "updated below" line. Ids of cards that left the transcript stay in the set harmlessly until the chat closes. Not persisted: it lasts while the chat is on screen (switching chats, a new window or a relaunch starts collapsed); persisting would mean a new field and a migration in `pins.json` or another file, not worth it for a view toggle.
- **Scroll position:** scrolled up, expanding grows the row downward and the row stays where it is. At the end of the transcript (the reader within 40 pt of it, always in a chat shorter than the window) the toggle runs in `Transaction.disclosure` and the end holds follow it, as for every disclosure at the end (`docs/flows/chat-transcript.md`, PLAN "Disclosures at the end"; on the Mac from `TranscriptScroll`'s size-change bottom anchor while it follows the end, which `DisclosureScrollTests` shows for message rows, not yet for a card; on iOS from the end-moved-away hold, read from the code, not re-measured for cards): the newest card opens upward with its last line right above the composer, and a card taller than the window pushes its own row above the top, so the reader scrolls up to its start, as for a long work log opened there. Collapsing from the chevron keeps the row in place (the chevron is in the row, so the row is on screen). There is no collapse from inside a long expanded card; the user scrolls up to the row, as in Reminders.
- **Streaming preview, failed and lost cards:** no chevron (the streaming preview is not a finished card; a failed card has its own "Couldn't display" disclosure; an update line has no card).
- **Pinned card, the sheet and requests:** unchanged on iPhone and iPad. Opening the sheet does not collapse the inline card and vice versa; a request card closing the sheet leaves the inline card expanded. On the Mac a request card arriving leaves an expanded card as it is, and the pinned chip opens the card in a sheet (`docs/flows/pinned-card.md`).
- **Search:** a search jump into a turn with an expanded card highlights the turn as before; the card's state is unchanged.
- **Surfaces:** Bot Chat and rooms on iPhone, iPad and Mac, light and dark; the Other Conversations sheet's `ChatView` owns its own set; each Mac chat window has its own (a toggle in one window does not expand the card in another, like the open sheet).
- **VoiceOver and keys (iPhone, iPad; the Mac in "The Mac"):** the disclosure button is labelled "Expand" or "Collapse" with the card title as its value; the row's button also carries an "Expand Card" / "Collapse Card" accessibility action. With a hardware keyboard on iPad the button is focusable and Space toggles it.
- **Width and Dynamic Type:** the button keeps its hit area; the expanded card is the 560 pt max-width glass card, as cards were inline before compact cards, except that it grows to fit a wider table (below).

## The Mac: cards expand in place, no popover

User feedback (2026-11-11, on a shopping-list card with a table): "that bar is too thick and I dont like the popover. the ios version is flawless though"; asked where the card should open: "what would apple do". iPhone and iPad keep everything above exactly (iPad keeps its popover); only the Mac changes, all inside `#if os(macOS)`.

Why in place: the HIG says popovers are for "small amounts of information" and "Don't make it too big"; a 440 x 560 pt card with a horizontally scrolling table is neither. The inspector was ruled out: its ideal width is 340 pt (`BotInspector.inspectorWidth`), narrower than the popover, and the Other Conversations sheet has no inspector. A window-modal sheet was ruled out by the user (it blocks asking follow-ups). Expanding in place already exists, is the full 560 pt card, and works in every `ChatView` host (Bot Chat, rooms, Mac chat windows, the Other Conversations sheet), and Reminders, the precedent for expanding in place, shows subtasks where they are.

- **The row is the disclosure control.** A click anywhere on the preview row expands or collapses the card (`withTransaction(.disclosure(.smooth))`, as the chevron does on iOS). With no popover the row has no other action, so a separate chevron button would be a second control doing the same thing next to the first; the HIG's disclosure button (points down when hidden, up when visible, placed by the content it reveals, at most one per view) is here the whole row. The `chevron.down` stays at the trailing edge as the row's state indicator, rotating 180 degrees while expanded, now inside the row's button and hidden from VoiceOver; it is the only sign that the row expands.
- **One focus stop:** Tab (with full keyboard navigation) focuses the row; Space toggles it. VoiceOver reads the title as the label, the subtitle (or "Card") as the value, and "Expands the card" or "Collapses the card" as the hint; the separate "Expand Card" / "Collapse Card" accessibility action is not added on the Mac, since the row's own action is that toggle. Escape does nothing (nothing to close).
- **Context menu (right-click on the row):** Expand Card / Collapse Card, then Pin Card / Unpin Card, unchanged. With no popover, the row's context menu (plus the pinned chip's own and its X) is where Pin and Unpin live on the Mac.
- **Row metrics (slimmer, the "too thick" bar):** today about 68 pt (44 pt tile, 12 pt padding). On the Mac: a 32 pt tile and thumbnail and 10 pt padding, a 52 pt row. Sources: the HIG macOS text styles give Headline 13/16 pt and Subheadline 11/14 pt line heights, so the title and subtitle with the row's 2 pt spacing are 32 pt, measured as 32.0 pt in an offscreen `NSHostingView` (Xcode 27.2, macOS 27.0.1); the tile and thumbnail match that text block. The 10 pt padding is a native grouped `Form` row's inset, measured in the same host: two 32 pt rows sit 53 pt apart (10 pt above and below each, plus the 1 pt separator); `List(.inset)` and `.sidebar` rows sit 40 pt apart (4 pt), which is a list, not a standalone card. Only the vertical inset was measured; the 10 pt sides are taken equal to it, not measured. All values stay `@ScaledMetric`. No "UI values (locked)" line names the preview.
- **Shape (reviewer, 2026-11-11):** on the Mac the glass is a rounded rect with 18 pt corners, collapsed and expanded alike, and the tile and thumbnail are 32 pt rounded squares with 8 pt corners, concentric with it (8 pt + 10 pt padding = 18 pt, the HIG rule of subtracting the margin from the outer radius). Not a capsule: the 28 pt corners would clamp to a capsule at 52 pt, and on macOS 27 capsules are controls (the composer, the pinned chip); three glass capsules stacked at the bottom of a narrow window would repeat v0.1.49's "second composer" (`docs/flows/pinned-card.md` History), and a capsule morphing into a rect on expand is not how a disclosure behaves. iOS keeps its 28 pt card and 12 pt tile corners.
- **Header and hero image:** the inline card hides the Header the row already shows (`hiddenHeader`, as on iOS), so on the Mac a hero image is only ever the row's 32 pt round thumbnail; there is no other place that draws it. Accepted: the Header's hero is a decorative picture of what the card is about, the card's content (lists, tables, images in the body) all draws, and showing the Header inline would repeat the title, subtitle and symbol right under the row.
- **Scroll position:** as in "Expanding a card in place": scrolled up the row stays put and the card grows downward; at the end the end holds follow the toggle, so the newest card opens upward above the composer and a card taller than the window starts above the top (the reader scrolls up to it; Jump to Latest is not shown because the reader is at the end). This is the most common Mac click (the newest card is usually at the end); it is the established disclosure rule and keeps the latest line of the conversation above the composer, as Messages does when content arrives at the end.
- **Long card (a table wider than the column, a long recipe):** the expanded card is the same inline surface as today: 560 pt max width, or wider when a table needs it (below), the table scrolling horizontally inside it only when it is wider than the column, the transcript scrolling vertically over it; the inline card puts no minimum on the window (it is inside the scroll view), so the window minimum and the Mac layout probe at `width:0` are unchanged.
- **Wide table (user feedback 2026-11-12 on v0.1.65, "the gigantic scrollbar, it not expanding more when it can"):** an expanded card grows to its widest table's natural width plus the card's 20 pt margins, up to the column; collapsed previews and cards without a table stay at 560 pt. `CrewTable` publishes the grid's natural width (a hidden `fixedSize` copy) through the `TableWidth` preference and the card reads it into its max width; measuring is needed because the card's ideal width also counts every wrapping text at its single-line width, so a `fixedSize` or `ViewThatFits` card would balloon to the column for any long paragraph. The table itself is `ViewThatFits`: the grid when it fits, else the grid in a horizontal scroll view; only the last column is flexible and takes the spare width, so the grid never lays out wider than its proposal (with every column flexible, `Grid` split the width evenly and a long column pushed the grid past the card, which likely made the table in the user's screenshot wider than its card).
- **Streaming preview:** dimmed, not clickable, no chevron (unchanged). **Failed card:** its own "Couldn't display" disclosure (unchanged). **Update lines:** no row, nothing to expand.
- **Replies back:** a button in the inline card sends the hidden action as today. Scrolled to the end, the reply (and a re-created card at its new turn) follows the end as any reply does. Scrolled up (a card expanded mid-transcript), nothing moves the reader, as for every reply while scrolled up: Jump to Latest shows and takes them to the reply; a re-created card appears there already expanded (same surface id), while the older turn shows "updated below".
- **A request card arrives (approval, question):** nothing closes; an expanded card stays expanded, and the request takes the composer bar as today.
- **Card updates:** the expanded card is the live surface; an update redraws it in place and a re-created id stays expanded at its new place (keyed by surface id, as on iOS).
- **The pinned chip and View > Show Pinned Card:** open the card in a sheet on the chat's window, as on iPhone (`docs/flows/pinned-card.md` > Mac sheet); the transcript stays where it was.
- **Surfaces:** Bot Chat and rooms in the main window, Mac chat windows (each its own expanded set) and the Other Conversations sheet (its own `ChatView` and set; no Pin), light and dark (glass and semantic colours; the tile keeps white on the tone gradient).
- **Removed on the Mac** (all `#if os(iOS)` from now on, iOS and iPad compile them unchanged): `ChatView`'s `.popover`, its `openedCard` state and the `openedCard` environment value, `showsPinnedCard`, `cardSheet`, `chipFrame`, and the three `onChange` handlers that close the card or drop composer focus when a card opens, a request arrives or the surface leaves; `PinnedCardContent`'s macOS branch (its Pin row and `ViewThatFits`); the `pinnedCard` focused value's `Binding<Bool>` becomes a plain action (it no longer toggles anything). No snapshot fixture sets `openedCard`; `CarouselMacUITests` clicks the preview and now finds the tiles inline.
- **Back on the Mac for the pinned sheet (2026-11-12):** `openedCard` state, `showsPinnedCard`, `cardSheet`, the three `onChange` handlers and `PinnedCardContent` (a Mac branch with Unpin and Done in the sheet's bottom bar) compile on both platforms again; the Mac presents them with `.sheet`, opened only by the pinned chip and View > Show Pinned Card. The `openedCard` environment value, `chipFrame` and the popover stay iOS-only, so Mac transcript rows still expand inline.

## The card sheet

- **One open card per chat**, owned by `ChatView` (the screen container, per PLAN's presentation rule): `openedCard` (id plus anchor frame) is set by any transcript preview (with its frame) and by the pinned chip above the composer (no anchor: the popover follows the chip's current frame, `docs/flows/pinned-card.md`). One `.popover` with `.presentationCompactAdaptation(.sheet)`, so at most one card is open. While a card is open, a tap on another preview or on the chip changes only the card id and keeps the current anchor:
  - iPhone: medium and large detents, background interaction up to medium, as the pinned-card sheet today.
  - iPad: a popover anchored on the tapped preview (440 pt wide, at most 560 pt tall, the card scrolling inside), the pinned card's popover (user, 2026-11-01: the pinned behaviour is "perfect", and a modal sheet would block asking follow-ups). Tapping into the composer or elsewhere closes it; Escape closes it.
  - Mac: no sheet or popover; the card expands in place ("The Mac" below).
  - The popover is presented by `ChatView` (the container) with `attachmentAnchor: .rect(...)` at the frame the preview reports in the chat's coordinate space, so no row presents anything and a recycled row cannot dismiss it.
- **Toolbar (iPhone sheet):** close (`Button(role: .close)`) and Pin/Unpin; on iPad the popover has the Pin/Unpin button in a small row at its top and no close button (popovers close by clicking outside or Escape), for that card when the chat can pin (Bot Chat and rooms). Unpin keeps the sheet open; Pin likewise.
- **Opening:** dismisses the keyboard first, like the pinned chip. Tapping another preview while the iPhone sheet sits at medium swaps the sheet to that card.
- **A request card arrives** (approval, question): the sheet closes so the request gets the attention, as pinned-card.md does for the popover today.
- **Content:** the live surface, scrolling, with the card's title as navigation title (iPhone, iPad) and the existing button-choice marks; disabled while a turn runs. Taps send the hidden action row as before and the reply streams behind the sheet.
- **The card changes while open:** follows the surface id (re-created or updated); closes when no surface with that id is left in the transcript, as today.

## Surfaces and directions

- **Bot Chat and rooms, iPhone, iPad and Mac, light and dark:** as above; the Mac expands cards in place.
- **Other Conversations sheet:** previews open the card sheet over that sheet (its `ChatView` presents it) on iPhone and iPad, and expand in place on the Mac; no Pin there (no pin key).
- **Mac chat windows:** each window's `ChatView` has its own expanded set.
- **Pinned card:** the chip above the composer (`docs/flows/pinned-card.md`) opens the same sheet; on the Mac it goes to the card in the transcript and expands it.
- **Bot list preview, time, unread, push, inspector:** unchanged (they never showed cards).
- **Sender, receiver, replies back:** the bot sends the card; the user opens it, taps; the hidden action goes back and the bot's reply follows the card-update rules.

## States

- **No Header:** title from the first Text line, no summary, default tile.
- **Long title:** 2 lines then tail truncation; long subtitle: 1 line, tail truncation.
- **Failed card:** the "Couldn't display this card" chip, as before (no preview).
- **Offline:** the preview opens the sheet (expands on the Mac); the card's buttons behave as before.
- **Accessibility sizes:** the row grows with Dynamic Type; the tile and thumbnail are `@ScaledMetric`.
