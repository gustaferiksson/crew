# Sidebar strip on the Mac (replaces #143's compact rows)

Redo of #167 (reverted in #171). Amended 2026-10-25: the strip shows only while the inspector is closed; with the inspector open the sidebar keeps regular rows and its regular 240 pt minimum. See "Inspector" below.

Dragged narrow, the Mac sidebar turns into one column of avatar tiles with the name under each, like macOS 27 Messages. #143's compact rows (avatar and name on one line) are removed on both platforms; this replaces `docs/flows/compact-sidebar.md`, which is deleted with its renders.

## What Apple does (measured)

From a 2x screenshot of macOS 27 Messages with its sidebar dragged narrow:

- Column 94 pt wide. One column of tiles, 78 x 86 pt (a little taller than wide), 8 pt in from each side, no gap between tiles (86 pt from one avatar to the next).
- Avatar 40 pt (the regular row size, not larger), 15 pt below the tile top, centred. Name under it, about 11 to 12 pt regular, one line, centred, truncated at the tail ("DHL Sveri…"), secondary grey; white on the selected tile.
- Selection: an accent-filled rounded tile (about 20 pt corners), the sidebar list's own selection.
- No search field, no sidebar toolbar buttons, no separators, no section headers; pinned conversations are just the first tiles. Compose stays at the leading edge of the chat's toolbar.
- Between full width and the strip Messages snaps: below a threshold the column jumps to the strip, it never shows squeezed rows.

## Narrowest column: 98 pt, not 94

#167 measured SwiftUI's `NavigationSplitView` on macOS 27 flooring the sidebar's minimum at 140 pt whatever `navigationSplitViewColumnWidth` asks for. Measured again 2026-10-25 with the probe in an unlocked session, the column does go down to the requested 98 pt (`width:0` gives a 418 pt window: 98 + 320); the 140 pt floor was most likely a locked-session measurement (see PLAN.md, Inspector probe). Either way the tile is the same, because the list is framed to `stripWidth`. Messages is not built on `NavigationSplitView`. Crew keeps the documented API and no AppKit workaround. The tiles keep Messages' size anyway: in the strip the `List` is framed to `stripWidth` (98 pt) and centred in the column, so the system selection (inset 10 pt from the list's edges) is exactly a 78 x 86 pt tile, from the narrowest column up to the threshold. SwiftUI has no snapping API and no hysteresis, so the column keeps whatever width the user drags it to and the layout flips each time the width crosses the threshold (once per crossing, never per pixel).

## Rule (macOS)

- The sidebar column is `min stripWidth (98), ideal 320, max 400` while the inspector is closed, and `min 240, ideal 320, max 400` (main's values) while it is open. Window minimum with the inspector closed: the sidebar's current width (at least 98) + 320, so 418 pt at the narrowest (was 560); shrinking the window still narrows the sidebar; with it open 240 + 320 + 2 x its width (1240 at 340, as on main).
- One decision for the whole list, as #143: `BotsView` reads the sidebar column's frame (outside the strip's narrower `List`) with `.onGeometryChange(for: Bool.self) { $0.size.width < stripThreshold }` into `@State isNarrow`; the list is a strip when `isNarrow` and `BotsView`'s inspector flag (`showsBot`) is off. The flag alone, not "flag and a selection", so selecting, deselecting or deleting a bot never flips the sidebar or its minimum; with the flag left on and nothing selected (Cmd-click deselect, the selected bot deleted) no inspector shows and the sidebar keeps regular rows at 240 pt or wider until a bot is selected (its inspector shows again, as on main) and the inspector is closed, accepted. The probe has no deselect step, so this state is checked by hand.
- Threshold `stripThreshold` 240 pt since 2026-10-26 (user request: 280 collapsed too early; between 240 and 280 a long name truncates beside a wide status label, as with the inspector open). Original 280 pt from #143's derivation measured again: a regular row needs a readable name ("Ada Lovelace", headline, 85 pt) beside the widest status label ("Needs approval", 82 pt) and 4 pt spacing, 171 pt; a sidebar `List` row leaves the column width minus 106 pt for that text (16 pt list insets each side, 12 pt trailing row inset, 10 pt dot gutter, 40 pt avatar, 12 pt spacing), so a regular row stops working at about 277 pt. #143's 260 truncated that name beside "Needs approval" (it measured 154 pt). 320 (the default) is always regular.
- In the strip the `List` shows, in order: favourites, then each section's bots and groups in their usual order (`rows(in:)`, groups interleaved by recency in the unfiled section), then unfiled groups when there is no unfiled section. No section headers, no separators, no favourites grid. Every tile is a tagged `List` row, favourites included, so selection, keyboard and menus come from the `List`.
- The search field is not attached in the strip (`.searchable` sits only on the regular branch). So the `List` is rebuilt at each crossing: the selection is kept (it lives in `BotsView`), keyboard focus and the scroll offset are not (the list starts at the top, as a freshly opened list). The connection pill's 2 s timer and the message search task sit on the column, outside the rebuilt list, so a crossing doesn't reset the pill's delay. The search does not run in the strip (nothing shows its results); a query typed before narrowing is kept and searched again when the sidebar widens; the strip ignores it, shows every bot and never shows the search empty state.
- Scroll indicators are hidden in the strip (`.scrollIndicators(.never)`): a legacy (always shown) scroller would take 16 pt of the 98 pt list and clip the tiles. The strip still scrolls with the wheel, trackpad and keyboard.
- The system sidebar toggle stays (Messages shows none), except at the narrowest widths, where the sidebar's title bar has no room for it beside the window buttons (gone at 98 pt, see the render); View > Show/Hide Sidebar and Ctrl-Cmd-S work at every width, accepted. `.toolbar(removing: .sidebarToggle)` on the sidebar column (the documented placement) stops `NavigationSplitView` applying the column's ideal width, so every window opened with the sidebar at AppKit's default 144 pt, in the strip (measured in a hosted split view: 320 pt without the modifier, 144 with it, also unconditionally; on the split view itself it doesn't remove the toggle). #167 shipped it and the Mac UI tests' clean 960 pt window opened in the strip (runs 40094061245, 40097936897). No workaround: the toggle stays. New Chat stays at the leading edge of the chat column's toolbar.

## Tile

- `StripTile`: a fixed 78 x 86 pt frame (`stripTileSize`): avatar (`rowAvatarSize`, 40 pt) 15 pt from the top, 6 pt to the name, name in `.subheadline` (11 pt), `.secondary`, one line, tail truncation, centred, 4 pt side padding. Row insets zero, separator hidden. The sidebar `List` gives row content only 66 of its 98 pt even with zero row insets (16 pt in from each edge, measured 2026-10-26), centred on the 78 pt selection; the fixed tile is wider than that, and a row places content wider than its proposal at the leading edge (avatar centre 55 pt, the selection's 49), so `StripTile` ends in `.frame(minWidth: 0, maxWidth: .infinity)`, which takes the row's width and centres the tile in it (the tile overflows 6 pt each side, unclipped). A plain `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` does nothing: without `minWidth` it never gets narrower than its 78 pt child. Fixed points, like the rows; the System Settings sidebar icon size does not change it (Crew's rows ignore it too).
- Selection: the system sidebar selection of the row (accent fill with white text when the window is key, grey when not), not a painted tile. Its corner radius is the system's (about 8 to 10 pt, Messages' about 12). The renders are captured from an offscreen window that is not key, so they show the grey state.
- Status on the avatar, as #143's compact badge: working green `ellipsis` (variable colour effect, honours Reduce Motion), waiting (approval, question, payment) orange `exclamationmark`, failed red `xmark`, a 13 pt circle knocked out of the avatar with a 2 pt gap. Unread: the regular row's 8 pt accent dot, its centre 5 pt left of the avatar (where the regular row puts it), vertically centred on it.
- Groups: `GroupAvatar` and the room name; no status badge (rooms have none).
- Drafts: not shown in the tile (no room, Messages shows none); the composer keeps them and the regular row shows them again when widened.
- Hover: `.help` with the full "title · role" (or the room name), since the tile truncates the name and drops the role.
- VoiceOver: one element per tile (`children: .ignore`), label "title, role" (or the title; the room name for groups), value the status ("Working", "Needs approval", "Waiting for you", "Wants to pay", "Failed", "Unread") plus "Draft" when a draft is pending and the chat is not open (groups: "Draft" or nothing). The `List` adds "selected".

## Surfaces

- **Selection, keyboard:** arrow keys move through the tiles; Return and double click open the bot in its own window (`contextMenu(forSelectionType:primaryAction:)`), now also for favourites in the strip; ⌘O works from the selected bot as today. Crew has no Find command, so there is no ⌘F to define in the strip.
- **Context menus:** the `List`'s selection menu (Open in New Window, Pin/Unpin, Duplicate, Move to Section, Delete) on bot tiles, favourites included. Group tiles have no menu, as group rows today. The leading swipe to pin is iOS's; the Mac strip has none, as the Mac rows have none in practice.
- **Inspector:** opens from the persona pill or View > Show Inspector (Ctrl-Cmd-I) as before. Opening it with the sidebar in the strip turns the strip back into regular rows and raises the column to 240 pt (the window grows to its 1240 pt minimum, as on main); the search field returns, with any earlier query. Closing it returns the column to the width the user dragged (measured: `sidebar:200,open,close` logs 200, 240, 200) and the strip if that is below 240 pt. Keyboard focus in the sidebar does not survive the switch (the `List` is rebuilt, as at any crossing), accepted. Regular rows between 240 and 280 pt truncate a long name beside a wide status label, as on main, accepted. Dragging the divider below 240 pt while the inspector is open stops at 240 pt with regular rows, as on main. Why: in #167 the strip's sizes fed the split view while the inspector changed the window minimum, and opening the inspector in a 960 pt window crashed in a constraint loop (`_postWindowNeedsUpdateConstraints` from `NSHostingView.SizeConstraints.update`; main's ui.yml run 40101434807, reproduced offscreen 6 of 6 on the #167 code).
- **Chat, composer, push, chat windows:** untouched. A push that selects a bot selects its tile.
- **Connection pill:** too wide for the strip, so the strip shows `ConnectionBadge` instead: the pill's icon (spinner or orange `wifi.exclamationmark`) in a 36 pt glass circle, chosen by `isStrip` (the regular sidebar and iOS keep the full pill unchanged), with the state and host as its tooltip and VoiceOver label and value; while unreachable the circle is the Retry button (hint "Retry"), while connecting it is disabled.
- **Light and dark:** system selection, system badge colours, knockout instead of painted rings; rendered in both.

## States

- **Empty, loading, error:** "No Bots", the spinner and alerts are overlays of the list, unchanged (text wraps in the 98 pt list).
- **Offline:** cached tiles with cached statuses, the icon-only pill at the bottom after the usual 2 s.
- **Long names:** one line, tail truncated, full name in the tooltip; role never shown.
- **Inspector open:** regular rows at 240 pt or wider, see Surfaces.
- **Many bots:** the list scrolls; after a crossing it starts at the top (see Rule), the selection is kept.
- **Search active while narrowing:** see Rule; no empty-search view in the strip, results return on widening.

## iPadOS and iPhone

Messages on iPad has no strip; its rows simply get narrower. iPad keeps plain regular rows at every sidebar width (min 240, ideal 320); #143's iPad compact rows go. Accepted: in an iPad sidebar narrowed below about 300 pt a regular row truncates the name before the status label (the label is fixed size), as Messages on iPad truncates its names; at the default 320 nothing changes. iPhone is full width and unchanged.
